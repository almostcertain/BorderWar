Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

BorderWar is a free browser strategy game. This page explains what information the game handles when you play at borderwar.io, and what we do with it. The short version: we keep as little as we can, we don't run ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your information.

Playing without an account

You can play singleplayer and multiplayer without signing up. In that case:

  • Your player name and clan tag are whatever you type. In multiplayer they are sent to our server and shown to the other players in your lobby and match. Anyone can pick any name, so please don't use your real one.
  • A random browser ID is created once and sent when you join a multiplayer game, so a dropped connection can rejoin the same match. It isn't shown to other players and isn't linked to who you are.
  • Your moves in a multiplayer match (attacks, builds, alliances and so on) pass through our server and are relayed to the other players, because every player's browser runs the same simulation. The server holds them in memory while the match runs and does not keep them afterwards.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. If you make one, our server stores:

  • Your email address. It is only your sign-in name: it is never shown to other players, and we don't send email to it.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We can't read it.
  • Your display name, clan tag and game settings, so they follow you between devices.
  • The date the account was created, and your active sign-in sessions.
  • Results of multiplayer matches you play while signed in (win or loss, finishing place, map and mode), used for your own stats.

The IP address you connect from is used to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts, and is written to the server's log when you sign up or sign in so we can spot abuse.

What is stored in your browser

The game keeps a few things on your own device. They stay there unless noted above.

  • Local storage: your options, last-used name and tag, and the random browser ID.
  • Session storage: a short-lived token for rejoining the match you are in.
  • IndexedDB: replays of your recent matches. Replays are never uploaded; they leave your device only if you download the file and share it yourself.
  • One cookie, bw_session, set only when you sign in. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days and is removed when you sign out. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

You can clear all of this at any time through your browser's site-data settings.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics scripts, no third-party trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.
  • No in-game chat, so there are no messages to store.

Who else handles your data

The game server is run by the BorderWar developers and is reached through Cloudflare, which carries the traffic between your browser and the server and therefore sees your IP address and requests, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The links to Reddit, Discord and GitHub on the main menu take you to those sites, which have their own policies; nothing is sent to them unless you click through.

How long we keep things

  • Account information is kept until you delete the account or ask us to.
  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days without use.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
  • The server also keeps anonymous totals (how many players and games are online) to monitor its health. These contain nothing about individual players.

Your choices

You can change your name, tag and settings from your account in the game. To change your email or password, get a copy of your account information, or have your account and everything attached to it deleted, contact us and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law (such as the GDPR or CCPA); we'll honor those requests too.

Children

BorderWar isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: reach the developers on Discord, at r/BorderWar, or by opening an issue on GitHub. BorderWar's source code is public, so you can check any of the above for yourself.
